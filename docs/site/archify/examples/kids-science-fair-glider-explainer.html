How does the Paper Airplane Science Fair Lab turn flight trials into 3D visuals and a printable report?

Long image

A

Answer

Every hypothesis, stalled dead-end flight, balance discovery, and final wingtip decision is saved by NumberOneAgent in a tamper-evident local notebook — powering both a Three.js 3D Flight Lab and a 9-chapter Science Fair Documentary.

  1. agent.recordFailedAttempt() saves Trial #2's tail-weight stall (1.4m) so teammates never repeat the flop.1
  2. agent.captureLesson() records nose paperclip balance (9.4m) and symmetrical 1cm wingtips (10.6m).2
  3. agent.consult() warns classmates before retrying tail weights and surfaces verified aerodynamics tips.3
  4. scene.js renders all 4 parabolic flight curves and paper airplanes in Three.js with a live time-travel scrubber.4
