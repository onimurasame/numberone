How does the RoboHawks Safety Arena block unsafe motor speeds and rewind build history?

Long image

A

Answer

NumberOneAgent protects a school robotics team with an enforceable L2 indoor motor speed cap (<= 80%), verifies every driver plan through AuthorityEngine.arbitrate(), and replays build history step by step inside a Three.js 3D Robotics Arena.

  1. robot.issueDirective() registers a blocking L2 rule forbidding setSpeed(100) on indoor tables.1
  2. robot.arbitrate() immediately emits BLOCK for 100% throttle while approving both setSpeed(75) and a negation-aware compliant plan ("Do not use setSpeed(100)...").2
  3. robot.replay({ untilSeq }) powers the 3D Time-Travel slider so students watch foam rails and safety arches activate in chronological order.3
  4. scene.js drops a glowing crimson Three.js laser barrier whenever a plan is BLOCKed.4
